Our Services

The Watchlist — oversight for every stage of ownership.

Whatever stage your property is at, there's a way for us to be your eyes on the ground.

Is your property being maintained, or quietly exploited?

Most owners rely on an informal, unaccountable caretaker — a "site boy," a distant relative, or a self-appointed "agent" — with no professional standard and no structured reporting. Vergehold replaces that layer with an engineer-led, reportable service.

Primary focus right now

Home Watch

Ongoing maintenance oversight once your property is complete and occupied — or standing empty.

A finished building doesn't stop needing attention. Home Watch keeps a regular, scheduled eye on the physical condition of your property — so small maintenance issues get caught and reported before they become expensive repairs, and you always know exactly what state your home is in.

  • Independent Site Supervision & Quality Control
  • Property oversight & maintenance subscription (Borehole, water & power systems)
  • Drone-Based site Documentation
  • Snagging & Defects Inspection
  • Construction Progress Reporting for lenders
  • Diaspora property search & acquisition support
  • Virtual property tours (live video walkthroughs)
