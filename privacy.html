Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what personal information Vergehold (operated by [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME], "we", "us") collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

1. What we collect

The only personal information we collect is what you choose to type into the contact form on our Contact page:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone or WhatsApp number (optional)
  • The service you're interested in
  • The location of your property
  • Any message you write to us

We don't ask for payment details, government ID numbers, or any other sensitive information through this website. Please don't include any in your message.

2. How we use it

We use your details only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or inspection request
  • Follow up with you about the service you asked about
  • Keep a record of the conversation

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

3. Who handles your information

Your information passes through a few services in order to reach us:

  • Formspree processes the contact form. When you submit it, Formspree receives what you typed, stores it for a limited period, and forwards it to us by email. Formspree has its own privacy policy at formspree.io.
  • Our email provider stores the notification emails we receive, along with our replies to you.
  • Website hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most hosts, it may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit.
  • Content delivery services. To load page styles, icons, animations, and fonts, your browser fetches files from jsDelivr, unpkg, and Google Fonts. These services may receive your IP address and browser details as part of delivering those files.

We don't share your details with anyone else unless the law requires it.

4. Cookies and tracking

We don't currently use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies on this website. If that changes, we'll update this page and, where required, ask for your consent first.

5. How long we keep your information

Formspree keeps form submissions for a limited time. We keep the emails we receive from you for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and keep a reasonable record of it, and no longer than [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 12 months] after our last contact with you, unless you become a client and we need to keep records for longer.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. You can also withdraw your consent for us to contact you. To do any of these, email us at the address below. We handle personal information in line with applicable Nigerian data protection law.

7. Security

This website is served over HTTPS, so what you submit is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we can't promise absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed.
